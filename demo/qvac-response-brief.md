## Pass 1 — The Brief

### 1. Structural truth

Future Selves is the only AI product where the most sensitive data you'll ever write — your fears, your avoidance, your hope — is the *primary input*, not a side effect.

Substitute test: ChatGPT asks "what's on your mind." Future Selves asks "what are you afraid won't happen?" The data shape is different by design, not by accident. A journal app doesn't generate AI responses. A therapy app doesn't unlock voices over time. Future Selves does both, and that's the shape no competitor shares.

### 2. Formal constraint

Because the structural truth is about data sensitivity, this video must *show* the data leaving and then *show* it staying. The visual mechanic is the departure and the arrest — a stream of text that exits the frame, then freezes, then stays.

Formal constraint: "This video argues privacy-by-architecture by showing the same prompt two ways — leaving, then staying."

Single argument: "This video argues that the most intimate AI experience is the one whose data never leaves your hardware, by showing the same confession leaving a phone, then staying on it."

### 3. Authorial angle

This video argues that Future Selves is the rare product where the privacy trade-off *is* the product, not a feature — and that QVAC makes the trade-off disappear entirely.

Why the brand wouldn't say this: the current live build uses cloud AI. Acknowledging this is the vulnerability that makes the pivot compelling. The video shows the problem and the fix in the same frame.

### 4. Felt arc

Entry state: curiosity ("what is this?")
Exit state: conviction ("this is how AI should work")

Arc: curiosity → realization (the data leaves) → relief (the data stays) → conviction (this is the standard)

Scene budget: 3 scenes (30s / 10 = 3 max)

- Scene 1 (8s): The confession. One line of kinetic type appears word by word: "What are you afraid won't happen?" The words float upward and exit frame — data leaving.
- Scene 2 (14s): The arrest. Same words, but this time they freeze mid-flight. A gold border draws around them. Below: "LLM: LLAMA 3.2 1B · TTS: Chatterbox · STT: Parakeet" — all on-device.
- Scene 3 (8s): The proof. "Airplane mode. Still works." A small chip: "bytes uploaded: 0." Close with the product name and @qvac tag.
